Why a commercial license?

Reflex Quant is dual-licensed. The core code is AGPL-3.0, which means anyone who forks and modifies it must release their changes under the same license. This protects the project from being quietly absorbed into closed-source products.

If you want to use Reflex Quant in a proprietary product, run it as a service without releasing your modifications, or embed it in a larger closed-source system — you need a commercial license from us.

License terms (summary)

The full commercial license agreement is a 12-page legal document. The key terms, in plain English:

  • Term: 1, 2, or 3 years. Auto-renews unless cancelled 30 days before expiry.
  • Use cases: covered uses are listed in your tier. Anything not listed is not licensed.
  • Modifications: all proprietary modifications remain your IP. We don't ask for assignment.
  • Confidentiality: we keep your use confidential unless you opt in to a public reference.
  • Support: response SLAs vary by support tier. Dedicated engineer tier includes a quarterly on-site (or video) review.
  • Termination: either party can terminate at the end of term. No refunds on early termination (you keep what's already licensed for the term you paid for).
  • Warranty: we warrant the code runs as documented. No warranty of profitability.
  • Liability cap: total fees paid in the prior 12 months. Standard.

This summary is informational; the signed agreement governs. We use standard SaaS / licensing boilerplate (based on the Open Source Initiative's recommended pattern) and are happy to walk through any clause.

Why the prices are what they are

Two reasons we don't price this like a typical SaaS:

1. The system gets more valuable over time. The meta-loop proposes ~3 new checkers per week from real trading data. The longer you run it, the more domain knowledge accumulates in your local knowledge graph. Switching costs go up.

2. We don't want this in the wrong hands. AGPL-3.0 already stops the obvious abuse (closed-source SaaS forks). The commercial license is the legitimate path for organizations that need a proprietary use case — but we want it to be a meaningful conversation, not a free-for-all commodity. A $5k/yr indie floor and a $100k/yr firm tier signal that.

FAQ

Can I evaluate before buying?

Yes — the Reflex Quant showcase is a live, clickable demo with synthetic data. No account needed, runs offline in your browser.

What's the difference between AGPL-3.0 and the commercial license?

AGPL-3.0: free, but you must release your modifications under AGPL-3.0 if you deploy as a network service. Commercial: paid, no release requirement.

Do you offer academic pricing?

Yes — for accredited universities and not-for-profit research, the Indie tier is waived. Email us with your institution.

Can I switch tiers mid-contract?

Upgrades yes (prorated), downgrades at term renewal only.

What about consulting / custom integration?

Custom / OEM tier only. Starts at $50k for a 6-week engagement.

## 10. Product Hunt — long description

> ## The first self-aware AI for quant trading
>
> Most quant AIs add more model. Reflex Quant adds more ways to be wrong about itself.
>
> **How it works**
>
> 1. **Trade** — Reflex Quant generates signals the way you'd expect: news APIs, market data, an LLM to draft a thesis, a risk engine to size it.
> 2. **Reflect** — The moment a trade closes, a **7-layer reflection engine** audits the decision against **27 error codes** (E001–E027). These check things like single-source bias, shallow causal chains, evidence-grade mismatches, and inverse-pair coherence.
> 3. **Self-improve** — When every reasoning-logic checker passes but the trade still loses, the **meta-loop** catches its own blindspot and proposes its own next checker. **3 new checkers per week**, on average, from real trading data.
>
> **Numbers from our 7-day paper-trading run**
>
> - **59 trades** closed across gold, crude, EUR/USD, BTC, SPX, DXY
> - **73 errors caught** across 27 error codes
> - **12 meta-loop cycles** — 3 new checkers proposed by the system itself

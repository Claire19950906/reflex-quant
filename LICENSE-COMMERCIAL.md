## Who needs a Commercial License?

You need a commercial license if **any** of the following apply:

- You operate a **SaaS product** that uses Reflex Quant and you do **not**
  publish your modifications under AGPL-3.0
- You **embed** Reflex Quant into a proprietary trading platform you sell
- You **white-label** Reflex Quant and rebrand it as your own product
- You **redistribute** Reflex Quant as part of a bundled offering

You do **not** need a commercial license if:

- You use the AGPL-3.0 version internally at your company (even for profit)
- You contribute patches back upstream (under AGPL-3.0)
- You are an individual researcher / academic / hobbyist
- You fork the project and publish your fork under AGPL-3.0

When in doubt: **email us** — z2132743607@163.com — and we'll help you
figure out the right path.
